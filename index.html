Research Profile


I am an MSc student in Biotechnology and Genetic Engineering at Khulna University, Bangladesh. My research experience focuses on bacterial isolation, antimicrobial susceptibility, molecular characterization and genetic analysis.

I am particularly interested in understanding antimicrobial resistance mechanisms, bacterial pathogenicity and the application of genomic approaches to infectious disease research.

My long-term goal is to contribute to translational microbiology, antimicrobial drug discovery and public health research.

Research Projects.

Selected academic and laboratory research

01 / CURRENT RESEARCH

Campylobacter & Antimicrobial Resistance

Molecular detection of resistance genes and biofilm-forming ability in Campylobacter spp. isolated from poultry in Khulna, Bangladesh. The study investigates resistance determinants and biofilm characteristics in poultry-associated bacteria.

PCR · Bacterial Culture · AST · Biofilm Assay
02 / UNDERGRADUATE RESEARCH

Cattle Buccal Isolates & AMR Profiling

Phenotypic characterization and antimicrobial resistance profiling of bacterial isolates obtained from cattle buccal samples in Khulna, Bangladesh.

Isolation · Biochemical Tests · AST · Microbiology
03 / MOLECULAR GENETICS

Buffalo Mitochondrial DNA Analysis

Genetic diversity and maternal lineage analysis of buffalo populations using mitochondrial D-loop sequencing and population genetics approaches.

DNA Extraction · PCR · Sanger Sequencing · MEGA
04 / COLLABORATIVE RESEARCH

Environmental AMR Surveillance

Research on antimicrobial resistance in bacterial isolates associated with hospital wastewater and river environments, investigating environmental dissemination of resistance.

Microbiology · Antibiotic Susceptibility · Data Analysis
