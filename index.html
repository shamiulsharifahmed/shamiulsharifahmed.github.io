About Me

A researcher with practical experience in microbiology, antimicrobial susceptibility testing and molecular research, supported by academic and collaborative projects at Khulna University.

Research profile

I am pursuing an MSc in Biotechnology and Genetic Engineering at Khulna University. I work as a Research Assistant at the Animal Biotechnology Laboratory, where my responsibilities have included laboratory experiments, microbial culture, molecular assays, antibiotic resistance testing, biological sample handling, preparation of reagents and media, and support for laboratory classes.

My academic work spans poultry-associated Campylobacter, buffalo mitochondrial DNA, environmental antimicrobial resistance and bacterial isolates associated with urinary tract infections.
